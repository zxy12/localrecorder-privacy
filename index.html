Privacy Policy (Local Recorder)

Local Recorder respects and protects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share information.

Information Collection

Recording files, tags, bookmarks, favorites, and other such data are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Advertising Data (Third-Party Services)

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device information (such as device model and operating system version), network information (such as IP address), advertising identifiers (where permitted by the operating system), and ad impression and interaction data to serve ads and measure their performance.

Data Use

Recording data is used only for the app's local features, including recording, playback, and management. AdMob uses advertising data to display ads, control how often ads are shown, and perform statistical analysis.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Google Privacy Policy): https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

Microphone permission is required for recording. If you deny this permission, recording will not be available.

Notification permission is used to deliver voice reminders you set and to notify you when a recording automatically pauses. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device. A reminder may display the recording's name; you can control notification previews in your device settings. If you deny notification permission, reminders and automatic-pause notifications will not be delivered, but you can still record, save, and play audio.

When you choose to save an exported recording as a video to Photos, the app requests add-only photo library permission. This permission allows the app to add the exported video; it does not allow the app to read your existing photos or videos. If you deny this permission, saving the video to Photos will not be available, but recording, playback, and sharing the m4a audio file remain available.

You can change microphone, notification, and photo library permissions at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

This app does not proactively request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. If personalized ads are enabled in the future, you will be prompted to grant permission when required by the operating system.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
